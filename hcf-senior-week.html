Senior Week Events

The send-off between the last exam and Commencement, and what comes after it

Harvard College Fund
  • HCF Staff Directory
  • Giving and Recognition
  • Associates

The stretch between the last exam and Commencement, run by the Class Committee and the Program Marshals. It is the send-off: a week of events for the graduating class alone, and the last time it gathers before a lifetime in the alumni community.

The week itself

  • Senior OlympicsHouse against House in the Yard, the class competing one last time before it scatters.
  • Last Chance to DanceThe final all-class dance, the last night the whole year is in one room.
  • Night at the MuseumHarvard’s museums opened after hours for the graduating class alone.
  • Senior Talent ShowThe class performing for itself, one night, no auditions to speak of.
  • Movie Night at the Widener StepsA film projected onto Widener, with the Yard as the theatre.

Where the details land

  • Harvard College SeniorsThe alumni association’s hub for the graduating class.
  • CommencementThe week that follows, and the ceremony that closes it.
